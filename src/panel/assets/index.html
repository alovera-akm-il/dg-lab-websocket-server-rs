Presets are sourced from dglab-kit's official waveform library. "Coyote" patterns target Coyote 3.0 hardware (what V3 supports); "OVC" (Opossum) patterns are bundled for completeness but designed for different hardware, so they may not feel meaningful on a Coyote device. For anything not in this list, paste your own frame data into the custom field, and start at low strength. On V4, custom/preset text must use the "<prefix>:[...]" frame-array format — raw legacy strings aren't supported by V4's wire protocol.
